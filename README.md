## Brief history

Aceton peroxide (APEX) is an organic peroxide classified as  a primary explosive.
It has been used in homemade explosives (HMEs) and in terrorist attacks, owing to its facile preparation
from readily available commercial precursors; acetone, hydrogen peroxide.
Among these APEX, triaceton triperoxide (TATP) is the one  most frequently employed in terrorist attacks.

In 1895, The German chemist Richard Wolfenstein serendipitously obtained acetone peroxide, now widely known as
triacetone triperoxide (TATP), while invetigating the action of hydrogen peroxide on coniine, the toxic alkalid
traditionally associated with the death of Socrates.
His synthesis involved treating acetone with hydrogen peroxide in the presence of a drop of phosphoric acid,
indicating an acid-catalyzed condensation process. He also demonstrated that TATP decomposed completely into aceton
and hydrogen peroxide upon warming with dilute sulfuric acid.

![APEX](images/images-1.png)

In 1899, Baeyer and Villiger synthesized DATP while investigating the action of  Caro's reagent(H2SO5) on acetone.They firstly assigned it as a polymer of acetone peroxide because its melting poiint (132-133) differed
from Wolfstein report's(m.p 97 degree). In 1900,  molecular weight dtermination showed that the compound was
in fact a dimer of acetone peroxide (DATP).They also reported that TATP was formed in the presence of concentrated hydrochloric acid,whereas a mixture of dimer and trimer was obtained in the presence of sulfuric acid monohydrate.

## Synthetic procedure

### TATP and DATP
Wolffenstein's synthesis has been employed for TATP. However, this method often affords a mixture of TATP and DATP.Therefore,many reserchers have attempted to modify it for optimaization.The yields of TATP and DATP depends on multiple factors, including the temperture,the type of acid such as HCl, H2SO4,H3PO4, and p-TsOH,
the concentration and molar ratio of acids and acetone, and the type of solvent (protic or aprotic).
Although I searched for a reliable synthetic procedure, most of the reports I found were of questionable credibility and lacked yield data or sufficient experimental detail. Here, I provides a reliable method to obtain the pure DATP and TATP separately.

TATP:![APEX](images/images-4-1.png)
Acetone (5.6 g, 0.1 mol) was mixed with hydrogen
peroxide (30%, 0.1 mol), and the mixture was cooled to 0 °C.
Concentrated sulfuric acid (5 drops) was slowly added at the same
temperature, and the mixture was allowed to reach room temperature
and kept at this temperature without stirring for 24 h. The resultant
precipitate was collected by filtration with suction and air-dried to afford
TATP (4.7 g, 65%) in the form of white crystals.

DATP:![APEX](images/images-4-2.png)
A mixture of acetone (62.5 mL, 0.85 mol), meth-
anesulfonic acid (3 mL), and dichloromethane (250 mL) was vigorously
stirred at room temperature in a 1 L flask for 10 min and then cooled
to -5 °C. Hydrogen peroxide (30% in water, 90 mL, 0.88 mol) was
slowly added over a period of 15 min. The mixture was stirred at the
same temperature for an additional 30 min and then cooled to -20 °C
and transferred to a separatory funnel. The organic layer was collected
and dried over molecular sieves (3 Å, 50 g, activated at 150 °C for 24 h) for 10 min and then filtered.
Dichloromethane (175 ml) and methanesulfonic acid (35 mL) were stirred in a 2 L flask and then
cooled to -5 °C. The above-described dry mixture was added, and the
combined mixture was stirred for 40 min. The mixture was then
transferred into a separatory funnel and washed three times with ice-
water (600 mL). The solvent was removed under reduced pressure to
give 24 g of a white powder. This crude product was recrystallized
from hot methanol (12 mL of methanol per 1 g of crude 2) and dried
under reduced pressure (2 mmHg) to give pure 2 (20.5 g, 32%).

### TerATP
In contrast,a single procedure has been reported for the synthesis of terATP using a Lewis acid, SnCl4 5H2O, and.
to my knowledge, hardly any subsequent studies have followed this method. The only reports I found were a patent published in 2011 and a journal article.
The patent did not provide reliable characterization data such as NMR, element analysis, or mass spectrometer,
offering only IR data. The article did not describe the synthesis procedure in detail and lacked a purification
procedure. I have doubts about its reproducibility. Moreover, according to the article, the choice of Lewis acid catalyst
made no difference compared with the other acid catalysts, and TATP was always obtained as the main product rather than TertATP. This observation further supports my doubts about the reliability of this method.
For now, the original synthetic method and the patent method are described here.

TertATP: ![APEX](images/images-4-3..png)
Acetone(8.12 g, 0.14 mol) was added to a solution of SnCl4 5H2O(350 mg, 1 mmol) in 20 ml 30% hydrogen peroxide and the mixture was vigorously stirred
with a magnetic stirrer at room temperture fr 24h. A white solid was produced gradually.
After the reaction was stopped, the unreacted acetone was evaporated on rotary evaporator at 25-30 C,
then the solid was filtered off, and washed with water. The solid thus obtained was recrystalized from a %50
acetone aqueous solution or benzene(4.67, 45%).
